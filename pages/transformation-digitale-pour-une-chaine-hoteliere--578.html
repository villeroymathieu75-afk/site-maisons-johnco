9-Month Digital Transformation

For a Luxury Hotel Chain

Case Study

Project Overview

This 9-month project aimed to digitally transform a hotel chain, enhancing international visibility and reducing dependency on third-party booking platforms (OTAs). The comprehensive strategy included website redesign, international SEO, direct booking incentives, and targeted social campaigns.

The transformation resulted in significant performance improvements across all digital channels, establishing a foundation for sustainable growth and improved customer relationships.
