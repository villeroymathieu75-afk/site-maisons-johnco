DNVB Fashion E-Commerce Transformation

8-month optimization project that increased revenue by 78% through UX redesign, funnel optimization, and data-driven retargeting

E-Commerce UX Optimization Retargeting

Project Overview

This 8-month project aimed to enhance the digital performance of a fashion DNVB (Digital Native Vertical Brand) by improving user experience, sales funnel efficiency, and retargeting campaign relevance.

Key challenges included a complex navigation system, insufficient product details, and a checkout process with high abandonment rates.

Core Objectives

  • Increase conversion rate sustainably
  • Reduce customer acquisition cost
  • Maximize e-commerce profitability
  • Reinforce premium brand image

Phase 1

UX/UI Redesign

A comprehensive audit revealed friction points: complex navigation, insufficient product details, and lack of key information accessibility.

  • Minimalist, immersive interface with HD visuals
  • Intelligent filter engine (sizes, colors, collections)
  • Enhanced product pages with customer reviews and recommendations
  • Interactive sizing guide

Results

Bounce rate ↓34%, Session duration ↑52%

Phase 2

Checkout Funnel Optimization

The original checkout process had too many steps and high cart abandonment. The new flow was streamlined to three clicks maximum.

Key Improvements:

  • 1
    Add to cart → Checkout → Payment
  • 2
    Diversified payment options (cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Klarna)
  • 3
    Urgency notifications (low stock, express delivery)

Results

Conversion rate ↑61%

Phase 3

Multichannel Retargeting

Visitors who didn't complete purchases were retargeted through dynamic ads on Facebook, Instagram, and Google Display.

Personalized Campaigns

Ads customized based on viewed products

Automated Emails

Cart abandonment sequences, personalized recommendations

Cross-Device Tracking

Following users from mobile research to desktop purchase

Results

48% more sales from retargeting (27% of total revenue)

Phase 4

Conversion Analytics

An advanced conversion analysis system was implemented using Google Analytics 4, Tag Manager, and Hotjar.

Tracking Capabilities:

  • User behavior (clicks, scrolls, time on page)
  • Regular A/B testing (landing pages, CTAs, promotions)
  • Continuous optimization based on data insights

Results

22% increase in average order value
